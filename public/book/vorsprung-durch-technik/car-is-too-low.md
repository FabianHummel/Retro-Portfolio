# The car is too low _(guess by how much!)_

![TÜV Austria](car-is-too-low/tuev-austria.webp)

Being the good Samaritan I am, I naturally took the car for its TÜV inspection after installing the lowering springs to get an official permit.

I originally thought nothing of it, because the springs had a German certificate and are practically impossible to fail during inspection, but seemingly my car is different.

The car did **not** pass inspection, because it was too low by ... _drum roll_ ... **1 centimeter!** One single f*cking centimeter, which is going to cost me an additional kidney! The requirement on how low a vehicle can be is exactly the same between Austria and Germany, but here in Austria the car apparently is too low?! Heck, the car was even manufactured in Germany, there's no difference!

I probably would've passed the inspection if I hadn't topped the car with fuel before, or removed some shit from my trunk. _So annoying._
