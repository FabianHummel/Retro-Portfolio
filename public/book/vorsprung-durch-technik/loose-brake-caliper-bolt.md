# Loose brake caliper bolt

For some longer time now I always heard a light knocking sound coming from the right side of the vehicle whenever I pressed the brakes. I thought nothing much of it and wrote it off as the rear splash guard clashing on the brake disk, as this was a known problem after our big repair.

However, things escalated quickly when I was on my way on picking up a friend of mine. As I was cruising down the motorway I heard a loud clash followed by the sound of some metallic part being dropped on the ground. _» I definitely just lost something «_, I thought and pulled over on the emergency lane. I didn't notice anything and the car seemed to run fine, so I continued my ride to pick up my friend. As I reached the last few turns a dreadful sound appeared whenever I was touching the brakes and told my friend to listen closely. After braking s few more times the scratching sound intensified to a point where you feel the entire car rumbling around when braking ever so slightly, like breaking out of a drift. _It didn't sound good at all_. Before hopping onto the Autobahn again I decided to check out the car in detail and it turns out the sound came from the front axle instead.

## The bolt is missing entirely

I jacked up the car with the emergency tools in the trunk, took off the wheel and the first thing I saw was that the entire rim was severely scratching against the brake caliper - it was only held by a **single** bolt. _I lost that thing on the Autobahn..._ I just stood there in total disbelief; how could such a big bolt get loose? We didn't touch this part at all!

I parked the car on the sidewalk and ordered a new screw which I'll be installing in the next few days. Everything should be fine then :)

## Afterthoughts

This whole situation could've ended a lot worse after thinking about it for some time. Yes, the scenario is extremely rare and would've been super unfortunate, but if I had to do an emergency stop the caliper could've eaten itself into the rim, locking up the entire front axle and the car could've skidded into the nearest tree. I try to cope as best as I can, but I try to not take it that serious. I think without that little bit of luck we would be all dead by now for sure!
