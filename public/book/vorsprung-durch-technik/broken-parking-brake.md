# Broken parking brake

The car worked fine for quite some time, until something pretty unexpected happened. I was on my way to a friend's house and parked on the curb as usual and pulled the e-brake. I noticed I was parked a little too far on the sidewalk in a way that people couldn't go past my car, so I wanted to adjust my parking spot a little. Sat back in my car, turned the ignition and suddenly a warning light appeared that told me to check the brake system. Much worse, the e-brake couldn't be retracted again and was completely stuck on the left side...

I didn't think a lot about it and moved on, spending the entire day with my friends, hoping it'll fix itself after a few hours. Often such silly electrical issues go away after some time. Long story short:

## _It didn't fix itself..._

It was already very late, around one in the morning and the towing service needed at least 2 to 3 hours to pick up my car. I felt stressed and planned to resolve this issue on my own the next few days with another friend of mine with a car jack and some tools.

Very early the next day I receive a call from my buddy: The voluntary fire department needed my car to be moved **immediately**, because they would start preparing the spot for their annual "Marschwertung" (marching-music evaluation; something very typically Austrian). Of all spots in this town my car was parked exactly _there. Immovable._

I told my grandfather about the incident and we drove to my friend's house, hoping to somehow get the car moving again. To our luck, a mechanic next door noticed my problem and came right to help me out by providing important tools I didn't have. Eventually we disconnected the parking brake and the car was thankfully moving again.

I brought the car over to the official Audi workshop and they replaced the rear e-brake with a fresh one. And of course I sold my organs for that, but whatever needs to be done needs to be done I guess... ¯⁠\⁠_⁠(⁠ツ⁠)⁠_⁠/⁠¯
