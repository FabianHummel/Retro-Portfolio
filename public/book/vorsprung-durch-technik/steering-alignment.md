# Steering alignment

Because of the car's overhaul the alignment was pretty off now and had to be adjusted. And I wouldn't write an article about it if everything went smoothly ;)

I brought my car over to my local inspection center and a few days later I receive a call that the mechanic failed to adjust the steering axle. This is the third time a trained, professional mechanic failed on my car. You gotta love it - it's truly special!

I picked up my car, drove to my home-workshop (that one friend's house) and asked his uncle again to help me fix it. My argument was that the front wheel hub has already been swapped last year due to the lowering springs incident, so the screws should all be fresh and easy to get loose. At first he was quite hesitant (obviously), after everything he went through with my car already, but ultimately agreed to help me out.

This time, _(almost)_ everything went smoothly, with emphasis on _"almost"_. The steering axle was easily replaced, but the rubber cuff around the joint was made for the facelift version with rhe electrical servo motor instead of the fluid based one in my car. _That was my fault._ It just delays everything unnecessarily again.
