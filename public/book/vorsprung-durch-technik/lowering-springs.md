# Lowering Springs

So it all started with my first yearly TÜV inspection at a mechanic my dad knew personally. While the workshop was specialized in working on Hyundai's exclusively, they'd thankfully make an exception for my silly Audi (won't be the only time, I'll promise!).

As you may have guessed my car did **not** pass inspection due to severe rust on all suspension springs. _No big deal_ I thought, and bought beautiful Einfach lowering springs specifically made for my car with a **German** certificate - keep this in mind, this is going to get important later!

![Broken Control Arm](lowering-springs/broken-control-arm.webp?size=half&align=right)

A few days later and I receive a call from the workshop - in the process of removing the control arms of the wheel hub they ripped off the screw and completely teared the wheel hub apart... Disclaimer: I was not accusing them of being at fault here, they tried everything to open this thing up, but the corrosion of the metal fuses these parts together like nothing else.

Hyundai eventually replaced both front wheel hubs and related control arms, which cost me a fortune, but I had the "pickerl" and was allowed to drive for another year. This alone was already really painful, both mentally and financial, but should only be the beginning of a **MUCH** longer journey...
