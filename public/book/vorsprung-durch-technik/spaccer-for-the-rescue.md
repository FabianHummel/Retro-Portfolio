# Spaccer for the rescue

At the TÜV inspection center me and my dad met a hobby mechanic going through a similar journey with his project car and we vented about TÜV for quite some time. Eventually he told us about Spaccer, a German company that specialized in producing custom lowering and heightening adapters for any vehicles. I contacted them and requested a 12mm heightening adapter, which turned out to be a super high quality part with a custom certificate specifically for my car - probably totally overkill, but whatever. I am already deep in the hole.

Only after installing this adapter TÜV was finally happy and gave me an official permit for my lowering springs. (which I still had to enter in my registration papers for an additional €26,-)
