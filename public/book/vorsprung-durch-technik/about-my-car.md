# About my car

![My little big boy](audi-on-a-hill.webp?width=40%&align=right)

My little big boy is a 2009 Audi A4 B8 with a 2.0L TDI diesel engine pushing somewhat around 120 horsepower. This makes the car only three years younger than myself, which is kinda odd to think about.

I got the car when I was 17 years old, just before I started getting my driver's license, from a dealership in Vienna. The previous owner had taken good care of it aesthetically and it felt awesome to drive. Yes, it _did_ cost roughly ten grand, but it would have been worth it if the car weren't meant to make so many problems later on.

Despite that, I absolutely love everything aspect about this car, mainly because it's from an era where car manufacturers actually built good and enjoyable cars! It's undeniable that the automotive industry was at the peak of engineering, namely regarding the design, handling, and repairabil- _... wait, stop. Repairability is **NOT** on this list. More about that later though._
