# Silly Unity Games

I grew up in arguably the most "cooked" generation ever[^1] — Gen Z was shaped by the rise of smartphones and kids being glued to the screen constantly. However, this trend opened up many possibilities for us and sparked my interest in the digital / technical world.

Around the age of 12 I really enjoyed playing the few games that worked on my phone such as Minecraft PE, Terraria, Clash of Clans, etc - _it really was such a careless life back then..._ I quickly started to dig deeper and explore ways of scripting own additions into Minecraft with command blocks. This was only the beginning of something that would follow me for years to come. I still remember I had an adventure map, a jump'n'run and a playground world which makes me feel super nostalgic. Umfortunately I lost all of those worlds, probably when I switched to a new phone...

At the age of 14 I got my first laptop, where I started with "real" game development (next to playing Counter Strike :') ...). I downloaded Unity and watched way too many Brackeys videos which guided me through my very first ideas.

[^1]: 10 years later I now know Gen Alpha is **SO MUCH WORSE** than us... I am glad our generation actually turned out quite nicely given our circumstances!
