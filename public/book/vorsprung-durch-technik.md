# "Vorsprung durch Technik" _they said ..._

In this mini series I'll go over each and every hurdle I have had with my car in chronological order, so sit back, relax and enjoy my biggest (financial) pain I probably ever experienced.

## Briefly about me and my love for cars

In the early days of my life I often worked on mechanical and technical things with my grandfather, as he was a learnt technician at Volkswagen and he taught me a thing or two about cars. However, that was around the age of eight up until when I was fourteen, when he unfortunately passed due to medical problems. The garage ended up not being used a lot, because I was still in an age where I couldn't really work on things alone and didn't yet have "projects" such as my car to get my hands dirty. Essentially, I drifted off into software development, as this was my special field of interest.

Only at a later point in time during the first few years of HTL I gained back interest in cars and mechanical stuff in general through a friend of mine, because he was a bit older and already had gotten his driver's license. And boy was he ethusiastic about cars. He took me racing up and down the Kahlenberg in Vienna, we worked on his car during the summer, sometimes late hours deep into the night, during rain, during storm - essentially whenever we could.

A year or two later I eventually also got my driver's license at the age of 17 and it was clear for me I also wanted a cool car like him. Inexperienced little me mainly searched for old Beamers I could modify to my liking, but my parents thankfully stopped me and told me to get a reliable and secure car instead _(well, so they thought hehe)_. It's only then when I discovered Audi and immediately fell in love with their A4 sedans.

Fast forwarding to the present, it was probably my _worst decision in my entire life at the time._
