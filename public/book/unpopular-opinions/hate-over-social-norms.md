## Debunking common misconceptions about LGBTQ+ in general

First of all, most of these should already be clear for the most people, but I'll quickly go over them anyways. For furry specific stuff I have debunked common myths down below.

In most hate scenarios or rejective behaviour in general towards social minorities like LGBTQ+ and associated communities is undeniably lack of education. To give some examples, here are some of the most widely known misconceptions:

_**Members of the LGBTQ+ community are a threat to minors**_

I actually had this discussion with an acquaintance of mine recently, because they once have had a bad experience at the pride parade years ago - which is, by the way, a very common reason why hate and rejection is propagated in the first place. My opinion here may be a bit unpopular, but I think it's quite a double-edged sword and isn't as easy to explain. On one hand I think LGBTQ+ is an ideal place for self-expression and learning to accept all kinds of people. However, it's undeniable that these communities are inherently sexual / kinky and that is an important thing to keep in mind when expressing yourself in public. It's difficult to determine where to draw the line of what's alright what not, so I think it's best to use common sense (like everywhere) and keep overly explicit content locked down areas.

_**LGBTQ+ identities are a choice or a modern "trend"**_

While cases of homosexuality, bisexuality and other gender variations have been documented for thousands of years now (dating as far back as to ancient Egyptian civilization), I don't disagree with perceiving this movement as _"trendy"_ in our modern world. However I can't think of any logical reasons regarding why that opposes a problem to our society. It's not solely for seeking attention as we see at the next myth.

_**Members of the LGBTQ+ community are solely seeking for attention and want to stand out from the rest of the world**_

Again, this is more of a statement than an argument, and it's straight up delusional. The LGBTQ+ movement strongly advocates for equal rights, peace and provides a safe space for people who don't strictly fit into our conservative social construct. This doesn't solely apply to people with a different sexual orientation, but also disabled and neurodivergent people just to name a few.

## Misconceptions about the furry fandom specifically

_**The furry fandom is purely a sexual fetish**_

While there is a section in furry dedicated solely to fetishism, the fandom is **not** inherently sexual. But that applies to virtually any community, especially in the gaming, anime and roleplay scene (Overwatch, Genshin Impact, Pokémon) just to name a few - but these subcultures are irrelevant and uninteresting to the broad mass. Scientifically proven studies by the international anthropomorphic research project (FurScience) show that for the vast majority of members, the fandom is about creative expression through art, cosplay and fiction without sexual incentive.

_**Furries believe they are literally animals and use litter boxes**_

As childish as this sounds, I have actually personally heard this argument in a discussion before, which is honestly pretty shocking. And even though I can't take this statement serious at all, I'll still clarify: There is a clear distinction between a _furry_ (anyone with an interest or emotional connection to anthropomorphic animals) and a _therian_ / otherkin (someone not or only partially identifying as human). I can't speak for the therian community as I am clearly not part of it, but as weird as I think it is I still respect the decision and am not stirring up hatred. Also the "litter box" argument is mostly originating from an internet hoax and is already widely debunked.

_**Grown men cosplaying in cute animal costumes should tell everything about furries**_

We are speaking about pedophilism here and this statement is unfortunately also quite prevalent in our society. While there are a handful of documented cases of pedophiles in fur costumes, they were not participating in the fandom in the classical sense. The vast majority of people in the furry fandom create and provide a safe space for people of all kinds and ages and actively protect minors from predators.

_**Using real fur for fursuits is sickening**_

Yes, it absolutely is. And that's why the furry fandom strictly rejects any use of real fur for fursuits without tolerance. There are plenty of faux fur manufacturers who also provide real-looking fur to use for fursuits.
