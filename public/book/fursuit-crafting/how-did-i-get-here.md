# How did I get here?

Not too long ago I really got into the Furry fandom _(because of reasons...)_ and started to draw traditional art again, as you may already have seen at my [art gallery](#/gallery). As I went further down the road (I deliberately don't want to call it a hole), I soon attended my first convention and developed a strong interest in fursuiting. My Instagram For-You page also didn't help with that, as I was constantly confronted with amazing work of other fursuit makers and artists.
