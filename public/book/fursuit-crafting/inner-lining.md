# Adding Lining to the Head Base

To make the head a little bit more comfy on the inside and protect it from sweat, the first thing is to add a thin, breathable layer of fabric made from cotton to the inside of the base. I will also use this layer to glue or sew other items to it, such as an electric fan and rubber head strap.
