# DIY Glass Cabinet

How shameful it would be if the suit was just thrown in a dusty corner, eagerly hoping to be picked up again. _Not with Fabi_ - I want to honor him by giving him a very special place in my room.

![Dusty Ledge](diy-glass-cabinet/dusty-ledge.webp#desktop?align=right&border&class=half,hide)

Currently, he rests on a small ledge on top of a cabinet, where he'll probably collect dust over time. Don't get me wrong, the spot isn't all that bad, but way too "unspecial" for me. But I see an opportunity for improvement!

![Dusty Ledge](diy-glass-cabinet/dusty-ledge.webp#mobile?align=right&border&class=half,show)

Right next to the cabinet is my wardrobe. It consists of three parts, all with opaque chipboard doors. My plan is to repurpose the rightmost compartment to some kind of "showroom". There, I would display the entire suit (head, tail and paws) under soft, natural light and also protect it from collecting dust.

I don't want to replace my entire wardrobe for a different one with a glass door - instead I plan to replace one of the doors with a glass one. This requires me to buy a custom-made glass plate in the correct size and cutouts for hinges.

Amazon or other big resellers don't really sell what I needed, so I tried out several local online shops that could do that for me. Eventually I stumbled across [glas-online.at](glas-online.at), an Austrian glass manufacturer that produces custom glass plates with customizable cutouts, finishings, and a lot more. I measured it perfectly and did some research on which type of glass to use, how thick it needs to be to hold the weight, how wide the hinge-bridge must be at a minimum, the correct diameter for the pot hinges, etc... I feel like a carpenter now.
