# My Fursona

In the first few weeks during my involvement in the fandom I didn't actually have a concrete fursona. However, it was clear for me that I was going to be something canine related, be it a wolf, hyena or fox. Since I was a small child I was always amazed by these animals and developed a strong bond towards them.

## Intelligence

I am a fast learner and can apply learnt skills pretty quick, which is super useful in both my work-environment and for private projects. Being able to develop or sketch out things fast helps me iterate faster and catch logical errors earlier in the process.

## Social ambivalence

From a social perspective, I tend to follow a similar structure to foxes and wolves, namely embracing loneliness, but simultaneously requiring a strong sense of belonging and community. I can't live a long time being alone, but also need a break after being with friends or colleagues for multiple days in a row.
