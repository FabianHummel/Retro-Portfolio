# My Fursona

Since I was a small child I was always amazed by foxes and developed a strong bond towards these creatures. I think I was around the age of five when I started to draw all sorts of different animals, which followed me through my entire life up until now. It's only now that I realize that I have been unconsciously part of the fandom for years, without knowing what furries even were in the first place. In the first few weeks I also didn't have a concrete fursona yet. However, it was clear for me that I was going to be something canine related, be it a wolf, hyena, fox, or anything in-between. I didn't "choose" to be one of these species - their traits actually match quite well with my human persona!

## Intelligence

I am a fast learner and can apply learnt skills pretty quick, which is super useful in both my work-environment and for private projects. Being able to develop or sketch out things fast helps me iterate quicker and catch logical errors earlier in the process. This skill got me through school easily and is the main reason why I achieve my goals the way I do. Canine animals are also highly intelligent which helps them catch prey even in the most difficult environments.

## Social ambivalence

From a social perspective, I tend to follow a similar structure to foxes and wolves - one hand I enjoy and embrace loneliness, but on the other hand also craving a strong sense of belonging and community. I can't live a long time being alone, but also need a break after being with friends or colleagues for longer periods. When I'm alone in public, I mostly lurke and vanish in the crowd - with friends I can quickly become the loudest and most energized. I often feel like owning two completely separate personalities that don't mix together.
