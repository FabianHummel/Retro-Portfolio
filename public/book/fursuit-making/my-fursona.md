# My Fursona

Since I was a small child I was always amazed by these animals and developed a strong bond towards them. I think I was around the age of five when I started to draw animals, which followed me through my entire life up until now. It's only now that I realize that I have been unconsciously part of the fandom for years, without knowing what furries were. In the first few weeks during my involvement in the fandom I actually didn't have a concrete fursona yet. However, it was clear for me that I was going to be something canine related, be it a wolf, hyena or fox. I "chose" this animal category because their traits actually match quite well with my humane persona, for example:

## Intelligence

I am a fast learner and can apply learnt skills pretty quick, which is super useful in both my work-environment and for private projects. Being able to develop or sketch out things fast helps me iterate quicker and catch logical errors earlier in the process. This skill got me through school easily and is the main reason why I achieve my goals the way I do.

## Social ambivalence

From a social perspective, I tend to follow a similar structure to foxes and wolves, namely embracing loneliness, but simultaneously requiring a strong sense of belonging and community. I can't live a long time being alone, but also need a break after being with friends or colleagues for longer periods. When I'm alone in public, I mostly lurke and vanish in the crowd - with friends I can quickly become the loudest and most energized. I often feel like owning two completely separate personalities that don't mix together.
