# Small change of plans

_Well..._ I kind of fell in love with the colors of the tail I made earlier, so I questioned the initial design once more and am in a sort of inner conflict right now of which design I am going to make.

Fact is that the tail is now finished and I am currently not planning on making another one, especially because VIECC (Vienna Comic Con) is only a little over a month away (I want to go public suiting there!), so I need to save as much time as I can.

On one hand I want to create a design that is purely based on the tail's colors, which saves me lots of time and still looks awesome in my opinion - as I already said, I really fell in love with these colors because they mix so incredibly well:

![Blue Design](small-change-of-plans/blue-design.webp?align=center&style={"width":"100%","max-width":"600px"})

On the other hand I can also imagine incorporate the mint colors (seafoam & sherbet orange/pink) into the original design, but I am not sure if it'll be too much overall. _Some part of me **really** wants that orange in there though..._

![Mixed Design](small-change-of-plans/mixed-design.webp?align=center&style={"width":"100%","max-width":"600px"})

For reference, this is the original design, with slightly adjusted colors to look like the actual colors in real life. Sure, it looks good as well, but that seafoam is just unmatched! :3
