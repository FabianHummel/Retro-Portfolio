# Ordering (the correct) Fur

![Ivory and Mallard Faux Fur](ordering-again/ivory-and-mallard.webp?width=35%&align=right&border)

I already knew what I wanted to order, namely a color that's actually somewhat white _(and not pink)_ and the mallard dreamy fur from the sample card I ordered initially.

Actually ordering the fur was a bit of a hassle though because I only wanted 25cm of the mallard so I wouldn't have too much excess fur and safe some money at the same time. This means I had to order via the support team which unfortunately took a few days 😖. However, delivery was very fast and reliable this time!

The fur colors turned out really good time. I already knew that from Shannon's Dreamy-Fur Mallard because I had the sample card, but the Ivory looks awesome. Yes, it could be a little more creamy, but it's pleasing to the eye nevertheless.
