# Fur Color comparison

_Soooooo... I kind of messed up._ I ordered the fur as planned, namely the Sherbet Orange, Seafoam and Burgundy, but it turns out the colors are **way** off. The sherbet orange is not orange at all, but rather a very pastel pink, whereas burgundy is pretty purple (but still acceptable) and seafoam is **very** light. Also worth mentioning is that the brown on the far left is pretty borderline black, but whatever.

However, I did find out that different lighting conditions make a noticable difference (as also seen in the pictures), and in fact the colors do look better when it's a bit darker, but not in bright sunlight. 😔

## It's not the end of the day! _(yet)_

Now there are two options I am left with: Either I keep the distorted colors for my suit, or make an additional order of the correct fur colors. I thought about it for a while, but ultimately made the decision to buy the correct colors for an additional €70,- (I swear to god, those shipping fees are insane!). I didn't want to be left with a suit that I am not happy with, as I want it to look good and feel comfortable with it.

Don't get me wrong, the colors are still beautiful and super high quality, they just don't fit my needs, so I'll make use of them another way. My plan is to create premade accessories such as tails and paws and try to sell them for a cheap price. This helps me gain experience with sewing and patterning on a "practice" piece before getting my hands on the actual suit. I already won if I somehow retrieve the €70,- again so I am not loosing out on money.
