# Ducktape Dummy

![Ducktape Dummy](ducktape-dummy/ducktape-dummy.webp?width=200px&align=right&border)

Now that the base is (sort of) finished I can start wrapping the entire head in ducktape to create individual patterns for cutting out the fur later. In fact, I only need to wrap half of the head because my pattern is completely symmetrical. This not only saves time but also makes cutting out the fur quite a bit simpler.

I spent extra time making sure the tape really fits snug around and within the ears, as well as paying detail to capture every curve as best as possible. This step was actually pretty easy and went smoothly without any issues (thank goodness). The only problems I had were taping around the neck because of its complex curvature, but nothing terrible.

## Marking the different patterns

Next up was marking the design with a sharpie! I messed up quite often because I didn't have a refsheet of my fursona yet - if you look closely in the image below, you can actually see some of the the crossed out markings which totally didn't help keeping everything organized ;)

Also note the small arrows and letters on the tape, which indicate the fur direction and color! This information is crucial when cutting out the fur because I can 100% guarantee that you forget what pattern goes where in which direction. Normally you'd also write down the rough location of each part, but I don't have so many individual pieces that I need to keep track of that. There are designs with **way** more parts than mine.

## Cutting out each individual part

The last step here is to cut out each individual part, which resulted in quite a lot pieces! _That'll be quite the journey to cut out and sew... xD_ All in all, these steps took only a day or two to complete and I am definitely happy with the result.
