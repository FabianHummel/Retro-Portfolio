# Running Heurigen

![Running Heurigen](running-heurigen/bieber.webp?height=400px&align=right)

> _Die Running Heurigen OG wird von drei begeisterten Gesellschaftern betrieben, die es sich zur Aufgabe gemacht haben, gegen das Problem mangelnder traditioneller Küche in Großstädten Österreichs anzukämpfen. Ganz nach dem Motto Qualität statt Quantität!_

Running Heurigen is the business-project of mine and two friends as part of our business administration and management classes. I think the project started in 2022 during our third year of HTL, when we were tasked to create a business plan for a fictitious company. The idea of Running Heurigen originated from our love for traditional Austrian cuisine and decided to give it a modern twist by combining it with a "running-sushi"-like concept, _but with salads, breads and schnitzel._
